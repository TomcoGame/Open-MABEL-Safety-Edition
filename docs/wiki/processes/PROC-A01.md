# PROC-A01 — Safety Management Plan

**Status:** Draft for technical review · **Revision:** 0.1 · **Application:** Open MABEL Safety Edition · **Process owner:** To assign

> This is an original project procedure framework. Applicability and clause-level coverage must be checked against licensed standards and a defined robot configuration.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Establish the lifecycle governance for safety-related engineering decisions. Applies to industrial mobile manipulation reference configurations and is tailored when other application profiles are selected.

## 2. Normative references to assess

ISO 12100; ISO 10218-1/-2 when applicable; ISO 13849-1; IEC 62061 when selected. The [standards register](standards-register.csv) records editions and decisions; a listing here does not assert full applicability.

## 3. Entry criteria and inputs

Product scope, standards profile, risk register, project schedule. All inputs must identify configuration and revision.

## 4. Responsibilities

Safety owner, engineering lead, independent reviewer. The designated safety owner must confirm responsibilities and review independence before release.

## 5. Procedure

1. Define safety lifecycle and gates.
2. Appoint accountable safety owner and technical reviewers.
3. Establish independence and competence expectations.
4. Schedule hazard, requirements, architecture and validation reviews.
5. Escalate deviations and unresolved risks.
6. Maintain evidence and decision history.

## 6. Decision gates and acceptance criteria

Gate A: defined scope and roles; Gate B: reviewed risk controls; Gate C: verified evidence before release. Open assumptions and deviations must have named owners and disposition dates.

## 7. Outputs and controlled records

Safety plan, review calendar, responsibility and independence records. Store outputs in the main repository with identifiers, revisions, approval status and links to related hazards, requirements, architecture and tests.

## 8. Traceability and change control

Any change to the robot configuration, intended use, environment, safety function or applicable standard triggers an impact review. Link changed process outputs to the [master process register](process-register.csv) and associated safety work products.

## 9. Visual learning and supporting material

Use the [animated lifecycle map](../../assets/wiki/safety-lifecycle-animated.svg) and [static fallback](../../assets/wiki/safety-lifecycle-static.svg). Process-specific diagrams and worked examples will be reviewed before publication as engineering guidance.
