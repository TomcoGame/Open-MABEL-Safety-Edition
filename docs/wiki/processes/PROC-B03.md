# PROC-B03 — Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

**Status:** Draft for technical review · **Revision:** 0.1 · **Application:** Open MABEL Safety Edition · **Process owner:** To assign

> This is an original project procedure framework. Applicability and clause-level coverage must be checked against licensed standards and a defined robot configuration.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Identify hazardous situations and derive risk-reduction actions for a specific robot configuration. Applies to industrial mobile manipulation reference configurations and is tailored when other application profiles are selected.

## 2. Normative references to assess

ISO 12100:2010; ISO 10218 and ANSI/A3 R15.08 as applicable; ISO 13849-1 for related controls. The [standards register](standards-register.csv) records editions and decisions; a listing here does not assert full applicability.

## 3. Entry criteria and inputs

Approved item definition, operating modes, mechanical/electrical architecture, incidents. All inputs must identify configuration and revision.

## 4. Responsibilities

Risk assessment facilitator, subsystem owners, independent reviewer. The designated safety owner must confirm responsibilities and review independence before release.

## 5. Procedure

1. Define assessment scope and selected methodology.
2. Identify hazards from base, manipulators, lift, energy and controls.
3. Specify hazardous situations, initiating causes and exposed persons.
4. Estimate and evaluate risk using documented criteria.
5. Apply inherently safe design, safeguards and information-for-use hierarchy.
6. Derive candidate safety requirements and allocate owners.
7. Reassess residual risk and review open assumptions.

## 6. Decision gates and acceptance criteria

Each hazard has a context, rationale, control disposition and verification path. Open assumptions and deviations must have named owners and disposition dates.

## 7. Outputs and controlled records

Hazard register, risk evaluations, risk-reduction decisions and residual-risk actions. Store outputs in the main repository with identifiers, revisions, approval status and links to related hazards, requirements, architecture and tests.

## 8. Traceability and change control

Any change to the robot configuration, intended use, environment, safety function or applicable standard triggers an impact review. Link changed process outputs to the [master process register](process-register.csv) and associated safety work products.

## 9. Visual learning and supporting material

Use the [animated lifecycle map](../../assets/wiki/safety-lifecycle-animated.svg) and [static fallback](../../assets/wiki/safety-lifecycle-static.svg). Process-specific diagrams and worked examples will be reviewed before publication as engineering guidance.
