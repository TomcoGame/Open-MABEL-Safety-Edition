# PROC-A02 — Standards Applicability and Compliance Mapping

**Status:** Draft for technical review · **Revision:** 0.1 · **Application:** Open MABEL Safety Edition · **Process owner:** To assign

> This is an original project procedure framework. Applicability and clause-level coverage must be checked against licensed standards and a defined robot configuration.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Determine applicable normative requirements by robot configuration, application and jurisdiction. Applies to industrial mobile manipulation reference configurations and is tailored when other application profiles are selected.

## 2. Normative references to assess

ISO 12100:2010; ISO 10218-1/-2:2025; ISO 13849-1:2023; ANSI/A3 R15.08 series; IEC 60204-1; ISO 3691-4 as conditional. The [standards register](standards-register.csv) records editions and decisions; a listing here does not assert full applicability.

## 3. Entry criteria and inputs

Item definition, intended environment, customer and regulatory obligations. All inputs must identify configuration and revision.

## 4. Responsibilities

Safety owner, compliance lead, application integrator. The designated safety owner must confirm responsibilities and review independence before release.

## 5. Procedure

1. Identify application profiles: laboratory, industrial mobile manipulation, other.
2. List applicable regulations and type-A/B/C standards.
3. Record standard edition and publisher source.
4. Evaluate scope inclusions, exclusions and overlaps.
5. Select governing design and validation approach per safety function.
6. Record clause-to-process and evidence mappings after licensed text review.
7. Approve deviations and re-evaluate on configuration changes.

## 6. Decision gates and acceptance criteria

No standard marked fully applicable without scope review; no clause compliance asserted without licensed-text review. Open assumptions and deviations must have named owners and disposition dates.

## 7. Outputs and controlled records

Approved standards profile, edition register, applicability decisions and clause mapping backlog. Store outputs in the main repository with identifiers, revisions, approval status and links to related hazards, requirements, architecture and tests.

## 8. Traceability and change control

Any change to the robot configuration, intended use, environment, safety function or applicable standard triggers an impact review. Link changed process outputs to the [master process register](process-register.csv) and associated safety work products.

Use the [animated lifecycle map](../../assets/wiki/safety-lifecycle-animated.svg) and [static fallback](../../assets/wiki/safety-lifecycle-static.svg). Process-specific diagrams and worked examples will be reviewed before publication as engineering guidance.
